# Celo Brand Kit & Attribution

> Source: Official Celo Brand Kit — `https://celo.org/brand-kit` (owned by the Celo Foundation).
> Last updated: 2026-07-17.

This is the reference for anyone who wants to **reference Celo in their own project** —
put "Build on Celo" on a landing page, use the Celo wordmark or symbol, or match Celo's
colors and typography. The official brand kit is the **canonical, always-current source
of truth**; this file is a navigational + policy summary, not a copy of the assets.
Always send people to the live kit to download assets and confirm exact specs — the site
is interactive and its colors/fonts/downloads are not reproduced here on purpose.

- **"Build on Celo"** badges, lockups, or callouts

## Brand Policy — what you may and may not do

The Celo Foundation owns all Celo Brand Assets. The kit exists so the community can
"appropriately communicate Celo brand affiliation." Confirmed rules from the Brand Policy:

**You may not:**
- Register the trademark, a domain, a Celo Brand Asset, or any derivative of them.
- Modify Celo Brand Assets in any way — changing a design or color, or adding words or
  elements to them.

**You should:**
- Use the official wordmark/symbol **as provided**, straight from the kit — don't
  recreate, redraw, restyle, or recolor it.
- Read the live Brand Policy (`https://celo.org/brand-kit-policy`) for the full, current
  terms before shipping anything public-facing. Treat the policy page as authoritative
  over this summary.

## "Build on Celo" attribution — practical guidance

For the common partner ask ("how do I say I'm built on Celo?"):

- **Use the real assets.** Download the wordmark/symbol from the brand kit and use them
  unmodified. Don't typeset "Celo" yourself or approximate the mark.
- **Give it room.** Preserve clear space around the logo/symbol and don't crowd, stretch,
  or place it on a low-contrast background. See the Wordmark and Symbol sections for the
  exact clear-space and minimum-size rules.
- **Don't imply endorsement.** "Built on Celo" / "Powered by Celo" describes the tech
  you use. Do not imply a partnership, endorsement, or official status you don't have.
- **Link it.** Where practical, link the Celo mark to `https://celo.org`.
- **Pull colors and type from the source.** Match Celo's palette and fonts using the live
  **Color** and **Typography** sections — don't eyeball hex values or substitute fonts.
